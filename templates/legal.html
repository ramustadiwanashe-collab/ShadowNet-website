Information we collect

ShadowNet stores your username, email address, password hash, account creation time and the premium access state associated with your account. The application does not store your raw password.

Why we use it

We use account data to authenticate users, provide access to learning content, maintain account security and manage premium access.

Security

Passwords use Werkzeug password hashing. Sessions use HTTP-only and SameSite cookies. For production, set a strong SECRET_KEY, serve the site over HTTPS, enable secure cookies and place the app behind a trusted proxy with appropriate security headers.

Payments

When you connect a payment provider, do not store card numbers or CVV values in ShadowNet. Use the provider's hosted checkout or tokenization flow and only store the minimum verified billing state required for access control.

Data requests

Provide users with a practical way to request access, correction or deletion of their account data, subject to the laws that apply to your organization.
